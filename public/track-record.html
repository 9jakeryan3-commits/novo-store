The track record

NoVo makes ten claims in public. This page scores every one of them against its own logged history and shows the sample size. Nothing here is smoothed and nothing is chosen after the fact.

Claim one

The open’s bias

Scored open to close. Neutral calls are not scored.

Loading…
Claim two

The hourly audit calls the next hour

Each audit states a bias for the hour ahead, and is scored on that hour. Neutral calls are not scored.

Loading…
Claim three

The Line’s level-breaks hold

Two questions per fire: did price go the way the call said, and did the level hold behind it.

Loading…
Claim four

The close lands inside the expected move

A ±1σ band contains about 68% of a normal distribution. That is the number to beat.

Loading…
Claim five

The gamma flip changes how price behaves

Below the flip, dealer hedging runs with the move. If that is true, the same ticker should travel further in an hour when net gamma is negative.

Loading…
Claim six

Gravity pins price where it sits

Concentrated gamma dampens movement around itself, so the test is volatility rather than attraction: is the next hour quieter when price sits on gravity?

Loading…
Claim seven

The walls hold

Call wall as resistance, put wall as support, read against the extreme of the next hour — a wall pierced and recovered did not hold.

Loading…
Claim eight

Steep skew precedes a wider hour

Tested by percentile rather than level: when skew is steep for this ticker, does the next hour travel further?

Loading…
Claim nine

The squeeze scale means something

The signal escalates at 25, 50 and 75. A higher score should precede a bigger move.

Claim ten

The Market Pulse says something about tomorrow

One reading per session, scored against the next session.

How to read this

This is NoVo's own log, self-reported. It is not an independent audit and it is not a performance record of any trade — NoVo never takes a position, and nothing here says what price will do next.

Every number here carries its window and its sample size. If a claim stops holding, this page will say so.
