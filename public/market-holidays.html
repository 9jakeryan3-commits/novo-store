Why half-days matter for 0DTE

On an early-close day the cash session ends at 1:00 PM ET, not 4:00 — which means that day's 0DTE options settle at 1:00. If you're scalping same-day options, the whole clock compresses: theta, the final-hour gamma, and the close all arrive three hours early. Dealer-positioning tools that assume a 4:00 close can misread the tape after 1:00 on these days — NoVo's dealer map is half-day aware and freezes at the real close.

Regular market hours

The U.S. stock market's regular session is 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET, Monday through Friday, excluding the holidays above. Pre-market runs roughly 4:00–9:30 AM ET and after-hours 4:00–8:00 PM ET, but 0DTE index options trade in the regular session (and SPX until 4:15). When a holiday falls on a weekend, the exchange observes it on the nearest weekday — which is why Independence Day is observed Friday, July 3, 2026.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the stock market open today?
The U.S. stock market (NYSE and Nasdaq) is open 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET on weekdays, except the 10 holidays in 2026 listed above. It also closes early at 1:00 PM ET on two half-days: the day after Thanksgiving (Nov 27) and Christmas Eve (Dec 24).
What are the stock market holidays in 2026?
In 2026 the NYSE and Nasdaq are closed on New Year's Day (Jan 1), MLK Day (Jan 19), Presidents' Day (Feb 16), Good Friday (Apr 3), Memorial Day (May 25), Juneteenth (Jun 19), Independence Day observed (Jul 3), Labor Day (Sep 7), Thanksgiving (Nov 26) and Christmas (Dec 25).
What are the market half-days in 2026?
There are two early-close days in 2026, both ending at 1:00 PM ET: the day after Thanksgiving, Friday November 27, and Christmas Eve, Thursday December 24. On these days that session's 0DTE options settle at 1:00, not 4:00.
What time does the stock market close?
Regular close is 4:00 PM ET. On the two half-days (Nov 27 and Dec 24, 2026) it closes at 1:00 PM ET. SPX index options trade until 4:15 PM ET on regular days.
