What you see

The dealer's map — decoded

Institutional reads on SPY, QQQ & IWM — dealer positioning, live options flow, sweeps & block prints, and the gamma squeeze — computed off live options data and updated through the session. Every term is explained in plain English; you don't need a desk background to use it.

🧲

Net GEX · the regime

Positive = dealers dampen moves (grind / mean-revert). Negative = they amplify (moves extend). Know which market you're in before you enter.

🔀

Gamma Flip

The price where dealer hedging flips from dampening to amplifying. Cross it and the whole character of the tape changes.

🧱

Call & Put walls

The biggest gamma strikes — the pins price gets drawn to and the levels it reacts at. Resistance above, support below.

📐

Expected move

The ±1σ range the options market is pricing for the day and the week — your realistic playing field, VIX/VXN-anchored.

⚖️

Put/Call skew

Are traders paying up for downside protection or chasing upside? The market's fear-vs-greed tell, in vol points.

📊

Vanna & charm exposure

The second-order book: how dealer hedging shifts when volatility moves (vanna), and the delta bleeding out of it every day into expiry (charm).

🪐

Gravity · the damper

The gamma-weighted center of the dealer book — the level price goes quiet around in a positive-gamma regime. Over NoVo’s own log the next hour runs about half as wide when price sits on it. A high-probability mean-reversion target.

🌡️

Fast / Slow MM skew

Near-term skew vs the structural baseline — whether dealers' short-dated fear is spiking above, or fading below, the longer-dated read.

⚡

Gamma squeeze signal

A live 0–100 read on when dealers flip from dampening to amplifying — short gamma plus a break of the flip, where moves extend instead of mean-reverting. Live on the dashboard the moment it arms, for whichever ticker you’re watching.

📈

Options flow · call vs put demand

Which side the volume is building on across the chain, the day's put/call ratio, and the unusual-volume strikes — the crowd's positioning as it forms, live off the chain.

📈

Gamma by strike, through the session

The dealer map with a time axis: one column every few minutes, so you can see where gamma built, where it drained, and which strikes held it all day — not just where it sits right now.

🐋

Sweeps & blocks

Real order flow off the live print tape — aggressor-tagged sweeps (one order shredded across venues to get filled fast) and oversized block prints, for SPY, QQQ and IWM. The buy/sell footprint, not just volume totals — computed in-house off the tape, drawn straight into your dealer map.

Why did we drop into the close?
Price lost the opening-range low at 765.86 just after 14:00 and there was no shelf under it until the pre-market low at 764.15. Dealers are short gamma below the flip, so hedging sold into that move rather than absorbing it.

Why does IV collapse after CPI?
The print is the event the options were priced for. Once it lands, the uncertainty premium comes out of every strike at once — which is why a big move and falling IV happen together. Direction and uncertainty are separate things.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this trade signals?
No. Analyst is a structural read of dealer positioning — the map, the levels, and what tends to follow, with real-time per-ticker level-break and gamma-squeeze signals. It never tells you to “buy calls here”; it shows you where the market is on the map and you make the call.
What exactly do I get?
The live SPY/QQQ/IWM dealer map — net GEX, the gamma flip, dealer walls, gravity, expected move, skew, fast/slow MM skew, vanna and charm — in an installable members dashboard; a written desk-note read emailed each session (the Open, the Close, and a Sunday Week Ahead); real-time ‘The Line’ level-break playbooks live on the members dashboard; plus the private Analyst channels in the NoVo Discord.
How often will I hear from you?
A morning Open and an end-of-day Close each trading session, a Week Ahead on Sundays, and real-time ‘The Line’ alerts — when SPY, QQQ or IWM breaks or reclaims a major level, with a hold-vs-break playbook and the confirmation to watch. Only when a level actually gives way, capped to one per level a day. Actionable, not a firehose.
Do I need a brokerage account or any setup?
No. Analyst is pure read — nothing to install, no broker keys to connect. You just open the dashboard and read. (Connecting your own broker so NoVo can execute is Trader, the separate automation tier.)
