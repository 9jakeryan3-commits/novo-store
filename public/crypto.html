Common questions

Before you start the trial.

Which coins does it cover?
All 90 coins in the map. Dealer gamma by strike is on the 7 with a real options book — BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, AVAX and HYPE — and funding, open interest, liquidation flow and your true cost to trade run across the book.
What about memecoins and on-chain tokens?
150+ of them, on Solana and Robinhood Chain, refreshed every pass. They have no options book and no major-venue perp, so there is no gamma to draw — the read is liquidity structure: pooled depth summed across every pool a token runs, turnover against that depth, and how many wallets are buying versus selling. Every token is keyed on its contract address, not its ticker, because one ticker routinely covers several different tokens.
Where does the data come from?
Straight from the venues that make each market: the options book for gamma, greeks and the volatility index; six perp venues for funding and open interest, kept separate; the exchange tape for hourly bars with real volume; and the retail markup as disclosed. Nothing is scraped from a screenshot or averaged from an aggregator.
What is the gamma flip, in crypto?
The price where dealer hedging changes sign. Above it, hedging leans against the move and dampens it. Below it, hedging goes with the move and amplifies it. Crypto expires at 08:00 UTC every day, so that level is re-drawn far more often than it is in equities.
Is any of it free?
Current funding by venue, open interest, 24-hour liquidations and the BTC and ETH gamma read are free on the market data page, with no account.
Does it trade for me?
No. It is a map and an analyst. You keep every decision and every click.
