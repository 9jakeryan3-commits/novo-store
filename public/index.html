Why dealer positioning

Price does not move in a vacuum.

Someone is on the other side of every option you buy, and they hedge. That hedging is mechanical, it is large, and it leaves a footprint you can read before the move rather than after it.

What a chart tells you

Where price has already been. Every indicator on it is arithmetic on that same history, which is why five of them can all agree and all be late.

What the dealer map tells you

Where the hedging pressure sits right now — which prices dealers must defend, where their hedging flips from damping moves to amplifying them, and how far the options market thinks today can travel.

None of it is a prediction, and NoVo will never dress it up as one. It is context — the difference between guessing at a level and knowing why that level is there.

Common questions

The things people ask first.

Is NoVo a signal service?
No. NoVo does not send “buy this now” alerts. It maps live dealer positioning — net GEX, the gamma flip, the call and put walls, gravity and the expected move — and tells you what that structure has tended to mean. You decide what to do with it.
What is the difference between Analyst and Trader?
Analyst ($129/mo) is the intelligence: the live dealer map on SPY, QQQ and IWM, a written desk note every session, the options-flow read, and NoVo the AI market analyst to ask about any of it. Trader ($249/mo) includes all of that and adds one-click execution in your own broker with automated exits. Analyst is for reading the market; Trader is for trading it.
Does it trade for me?
No. Entry is always your click. Only the exits are automated — the profit targets, the trailing stop and the hard stop, by the rules you set. NoVo has no discretionary authority and never enters a position on its own.
Is my money safe?
NoVo is non-custodial. Your funds stay in your own brokerage account in your own name. NoVo uses broker API keys you generate; it can place and manage trades but cannot withdraw, transfer or move money. Keys are stored encrypted.
Which tickers, and why only three?
SPY, QQQ and IWM. Dealer positioning is only useful when the options volume behind it is deep enough to be meaningful, and these three carry it. Depth on three beats thin coverage of thousands — you pick your tape and trade its levels.
What can I use without paying?
The market data page and its fear gauges, the per-ticker SPY, QQQ and IWM gamma pages, Options 101, the 0DTE guide, all four calculators and the whole Journal. No account, no card.
Can I cancel?
Any time, in one click from your portal. Analyst starts with a 7-day free trial — cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. See the refund policy.
