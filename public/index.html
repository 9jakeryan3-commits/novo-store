Why dealer positioning

Price does not move in a vacuum.

Dealers do not take a view. They take the other side and neutralise it, which means their buying and selling is forced, predictable, and concentrated at particular prices. Those prices are the map.

What a chart tells you

Where price has already been. Every indicator on it is arithmetic on that same history, which is why five of them can all agree and all be late.

What the dealer map tells you

Where the hedging pressure sits right now, which prices dealers must defend, where their hedging flips from damping moves to amplifying them, and how far the options market thinks today can travel.

None of it is a prediction, and NoVo Options Trading will never dress it up as one. It is context — the difference between guessing at a level and knowing why that level is there.

What you are actually looking at

The dealer map, in plain English.

Options dealers hedge. Where they sit decides whether a move gets damped or amplified, and which prices behave like magnets. That is knowable, and it is what NoVo draws, every 60 seconds.

🧲

Net GEX · the regime

Positive = dealers dampen moves (grind / mean-revert). Negative = they amplify (moves extend). Know which market you're in before you enter.

🔀

Gamma Flip

The price where dealer hedging flips from dampening to amplifying. Cross it and the whole character of the tape changes.

🧱

Call & Put walls

The biggest gamma strikes — the pins price gets drawn to and the levels it reacts at. Resistance above, support below.

📐

Expected move

The ±1σ range the options market is pricing for the day and the week — your realistic playing field, VIX/VXN-anchored.

⚖️

Put/Call skew

Are traders paying up for downside protection or chasing upside? The market's fear-vs-greed tell, in vol points.

📊

Vanna & charm exposure

The second-order book: how dealer hedging shifts when volatility moves (vanna), and the delta bleeding out of it every day into expiry (charm).

🪐

Gravity · the damper

The gamma-weighted center of the dealer book — the level price goes quiet around in a positive-gamma regime. It pins rather than attracts — price sitting on it moves about half as far over the next hour.

🌡️

Fast / Slow MM skew

Near-term skew vs the structural baseline — whether dealers' short-dated fear is spiking above, or fading below, the longer-dated read.

⚡

Gamma squeeze signal

A live 0–100 read on when dealers flip from dampening to amplifying — short gamma plus a break of the flip, where moves extend instead of mean-reverting. Live on the dashboard the moment it arms, for whichever ticker you’re watching.

📈

Options flow · call vs put demand

Which side the volume is building on across the chain, the day's put/call ratio, and the unusual-volume strikes — the crowd's positioning as it forms, live off the chain.

🐋

Sweeps & blocks

Real order flow off the live print tape — aggressor-tagged sweeps (one order shredded across venues to get filled fast) and oversized block prints, for SPY, QQQ and IWM. The buy/sell footprint, not just volume totals — computed in-house off the tape, drawn straight into your dealer map.

No. NoVo Options Trading does not send “buy this now” alerts. It maps live dealer positioning — net GEX, the gamma flip, the call and put walls, gravity and the expected move — and tells you what that structure has tended to mean. You decide what to do with it.

No. NoVo Options Trading is a map and a read, not a broker. It shows you where dealer positioning sits and what that structure has tended to mean. Every decision, and every order, is yours.
