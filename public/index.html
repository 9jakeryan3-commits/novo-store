What you are actually looking at

The dealer map, in plain English.

Options dealers hedge. Where they are positioned decides whether a move gets damped or amplified, and which prices act like magnets. That is knowable, and it is what NoVo draws.

Net GEX — the regime
Positive and dealers damp the tape into a grind. Negative and they amplify it.
The gamma flip
The price where hedging switches from damping to amplifying. The line that matters most.
Call & put walls
The biggest gamma strikes — the prices that pin, cap and support.
Expected move
The ±1σ range the options market is pricing for the session.
Gravity
The gamma-weighted centre price keeps snapping back to.
Flow, sweeps & blocks
Which side the volume is leaning, off the live print tape, computed in-house.
