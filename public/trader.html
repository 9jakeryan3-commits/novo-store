Where does the data come from?

Every quote is age-checked before it is drawn, so a frozen feed is refused rather than rendered as a live number. A level is never quietly carried forward from a source that stopped answering.

What is the Analyst Score?
A 0–100 grade of the current setup’s quality, updated continuously from multiple proprietary signal components — structure, tape confirmation and the macro regime combined into one number on the Market Intel strip.

It is a read, not an instruction. It tells you how much of the structure is lining up right now, and the record beside it tells you what scores like this one have preceded.
How is the track record scored?
Against what price actually did, on claims the map made before the fact, not on hindsight. The hourly audit is graded, the expected-move band is checked against the close, and whether price went through the walls is recorded either way.

Every figure carries its sample size, and the losing ones stay in. You can read the whole thing at the track record before you buy anything.
