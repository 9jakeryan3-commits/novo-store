The best gamma exposure (GEX) tools in 2026

Dealer gamma is one of the biggest hidden forces on intraday price — it's why the tape pins some days and runs others. Here are the tools that map it in 2026, what each does, and who it's for. We build one of them, honestly ranked among the rest.

Gamma exposure (GEX) maps where options dealers are positioned — and dealer hedging is one of the biggest hidden forces on intraday price. Positive net GEX means dealers dampen moves (the tape pins and mean-reverts); negative GEX means they amplify them (moves extend). FAQ

Common questions

What is a gamma exposure (GEX) tool?
A GEX tool estimates how options dealers are positioned in gamma and where they'll have to hedge. It surfaces net gamma exposure, the gamma-flip level (where dealer hedging switches from dampening to amplifying moves) and the call/put walls that tend to cap and support price. Traders use it to anticipate where the tape pins and where it breaks.
What is the best GEX / gamma tool?
SpotGamma is the established standard with the widest coverage across thousands of equities. For dealer gamma on the index ETFs specifically — and if you want to trade off the map rather than just read it — NoVo is purpose-built, and refreshes the map every 60 seconds where most of this list is on minutes or snapshots. OptionsDepth and MenthorQ are strong index-focused analytics alternatives, and GammaLab is the budget pick.
Is gamma exposure data free?
Some sites show delayed or aggregate GEX for free, but live, intraday, per-ticker dealer-gamma maps are almost always paid — the data and compute behind them aren't cheap. NoVo's free market-data page shows public volatility gauges; the live dealer map itself is part of the paid Analyst tier.
Does gamma exposure predict the market?
It's a tendency, not a crystal ball. Positive net GEX makes ranges more likely to hold and price to mean-revert; negative GEX makes moves more likely to extend. But news, momentum and a gamma squeeze can override it — GEX is context that improves your odds, not a guarantee.
