What is the NoVo Market Pulse?

The Market Pulse is a single 0–100 read of market sentiment — 0 is Extreme Fear, 100 is Extreme Greed. It blends index-volatility percentiles (VIX, VXN, RVX) with S&P price momentum, all from public data. It's a weather report, not a trade signal: a low number means the tape is fearful and stretched, a high number means complacent and extended. For the structure under the sentiment — how dealer gamma is positioning the market — see NoVo Analyst or read The Journal.
