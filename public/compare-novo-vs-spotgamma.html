What SpotGamma does well

SpotGamma has been the retail standard for dealer-positioning since 2020, and its edge is breadth. Its Equity Hub maps gamma on thousands of individual stocks, not just the indices, and its TRACE heatmap and HIRO real-time flow are well-regarded. If you trade single-name equity options — AAPL, NVDA, TSLA — SpotGamma covers ground NoVo simply doesn't. It's the more mature, more proven brand, and for a pure-analytics user with a wide watchlist that matters.

Where NoVo is different

NoVo is built for one job — 0DTE scalping on the three most-traded index ETFs — and it does two things SpotGamma doesn't. First, its data tier ($129) includes a live options-flow tape that SpotGamma typically reserves for its top (~$299) tier, against a ~$99 entry that does not. Second, and more important: NoVo is the only one of the two that lets you trade the level in one click, in your own broker, with automated exits. SpotGamma shows you the map; NoVo shows you the map and acts on it. That fusion — dealer-map readout plus execution — is genuinely rare; most traders would otherwise stitch a data tool and a separate execution tool together by hand.

Which should you pick

If you scalp 0DTE on SPY/QQQ/IWM and want to act on the dealer map rather than just read it, NoVo is the closer fit — a 60-second map with execution built in. If you want dealer gamma across a wide universe of single-name stocks and don't need execution, SpotGamma covers far more ground. They're not really the same product: one is broad analytics, the other is a focused readout-plus-execution tool for index scalpers — and some traders happily use both. Is NoVo a SpotGamma alternative?
For 0DTE scalping on SPY, QQQ and IWM, yes — NoVo maps the same dealer positioning (net GEX, gamma flip, call/put walls, expected move), refreshes every 60 seconds, and uniquely lets you execute the trade in one click. For dealer gamma across thousands of single-name equities, SpotGamma covers far more tickers than NoVo does.
Does SpotGamma execute trades?
No. SpotGamma is analytics only — it shows you the dealer-positioning map but does not place trades. NoVo's Trader tier is the tool here that also executes: you click once and it places the option order in your own broker, then manages the exit automatically.
Which is cheaper, NoVo or SpotGamma?
NoVo Analyst is $129/mo versus SpotGamma's ~$99/mo entry (Essential), and NoVo includes a live options-flow tape that SpotGamma typically gates to its ~$299 Alpha tier. Can I use both NoVo and SpotGamma?
Yes, and some traders do — SpotGamma for broad single-name equity gamma across a large watchlist, and NoVo to actually scalp the index ETFs off the dealer map with one-click execution and automated exits. They solve different problems.
