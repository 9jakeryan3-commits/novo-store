Analyst vs Trader: Which NoVo Plan Actually Fits You

Two plans, one clean dividing line: do you want NoVo to help you read the market, or to read and execute? Here's how to tell which side of that line you're on.

NoVo Options Trading · Jul 18, 2026

NoVo comes in two tiers, and choosing well means being honest about how you actually trade. Analyst ($129/mo, $1,290/yr) is the intelligence; Trader ($249/mo, $2,490/yr) is the intelligence plus execution. The dividing line is simple: do you want to read, or read and act?

What Analyst gives you

Analyst is the full cockpit read: the live dealer-level map (gamma flip, call/put walls, gravity, expected move), the tape analysis, and the Analyst Score — everything you need to understand the structure. What it doesn't include is execution: you place your own trades in your own broker. Analyst fits the trader who has their own execution dialed in and wants NoVo's eyes — the market intelligence — not its hands.

What Trader adds

Trader adds the hands: one-click execution (strike selection, sizing, routing), the protective stop, the exit ladder. The AI is included. Trader fits the trader who wants the whole loop: read and execute with discipline built in, no fumbling the entry, no forgetting the stop, no white-knuckling the exit.

Analyst is the map; Trader is the map plus the wheel. Pick by asking whether your execution or your analysis is the part you most want handled.

How to choose honestly

If your weakness is reading the market — you execute fine but struggle to see the structure — Analyst may be enough. If your weakness is execution — you read okay but fumble entries, skip stops, or exit emotionally — Trader's automated discipline is the point, and likely worth the step up (see the cost-benefit breakdown). Many start on Analyst to learn the cockpit, then move to Trader when they want the execution. Both are month-to-month, so you can change as your needs do.
