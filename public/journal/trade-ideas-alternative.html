A Trade Ideas Alternative for Options Scalpers

Trade Ideas is a strong product aimed at a different job: finding stock setups across thousands of names. NoVo does not scan for names at all.

NoVo Options Trading · Jul 18, 2026

Independent comparison for prospective users. NoVo is not affiliated with the tools named. Features and pricing change — verify current specifics on each provider’s own site.

Trade Ideas is best known for AI-assisted real-time scanning of equities — surfacing candidates from a large universe based on statistical patterns. If your process is “find me something moving,” that is the category.

Finding a name vs. trading one tape

A scanner solves selection: which of thousands of names deserves attention. NoVo does not solve selection, because that decision is already made — you trade SPY. What is left is structure and timing, which is a different problem needing different inputs: dealer positioning rather than statistical screens.

Analytics vs. analytics + execution

NoVo is an execution cockpit: it maps the structure — net GEX regime, the gamma flip, call and put walls, gravity, expected move — and when you decide to take the trade, executes it in one click in your own brokerage account, with the strike selected, size set to your risk settings, a protective stop armed and the exit ladder attached.

A scanner narrows the universe. A dealer map tells you where the one instrument you already chose is likely to react — and NoVo attaches the trigger.

Where each fits

Choose the scanner if your edge is finding the right stock at the right moment across a wide universe. Choose NoVo if you have already committed to SPY and want depth plus execution rather than breadth.

Where NoVo is the wrong answer

NoVo covers 0DTE/1DTE on SPY, QQQ and IWM and nothing else. If you need multi-ticker screening, equities research, futures gamma or macro data, a dedicated platform is the better purchase. NoVo never enters a trade on its own — you click every entry — and it does not reduce market risk. Options carry substantial risk of loss, which is why paper mode exists.
