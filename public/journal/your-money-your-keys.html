Trust

Your Money, Your Keys

Ask the hard question of any "AI trading" product first: where does my money actually live? For most of them, the answer should make you walk away. For NoVo, it never leaves your own broker.

NoVo Options Trading · Jun 28, 2026
Part of the 0DTE SPY Options Scalping guide →

The automated-trading world splits cleanly into two camps. One camp hands you a sharper cockpit — better charts, scanners, alerts — and still makes you place every order by hand. The other flies the plane for you — but only if you wire your money into their black box and trust something you can't see inside.

NoVo refuses both: it's the cockpit that executes for you, and it never takes your money. That combination is rare, and it's deliberate.

Non-custodial, by design

NoVo never takes custody of your funds. Your money sits in your own brokerage account, in your name, at a regulated US broker. NoVo connects to that account through the broker's own official API using keys that you generate — used for one thing only: placing trades in your own account. That's all it can do. It can't withdraw, it can't transfer, it can't move your money anywhere. There's no NoVo wallet, no "deposit to get started," no pooled fund.

Compare that to the typical autonomous "AI fund," where step one is sending your capital into their system. The day you do that, you've traded control for convenience and taken on counterparty risk on top of market risk. With NoVo, the only thing that ever leaves your hands is a trade order.

Powerful shouldn't mean handing over the keys. NoVo does the mechanics — but the account, the funds, and the keys stay yours.

You stay in control

NoVo isn't a black box that quietly moves your money around. Your funds stay in your own brokerage account the entire time — NoVo only connects through your keys to place trades. You can watch it work, halt it with one button, and pull your keys at any moment. There's no intermediary deciding what happens to your account — just rules you set, executed against a broker you chose. Your keys are used for exactly one thing: trading your account. Never to hold or move your money.

Transparency you can audit

Trust isn't a slogan; it's something you should be able to verify. NoVo keeps a complete trade journal — every entry and exit it has ever made, with the context behind each one. You can see exactly what it did and why on your own dashboard, and export the full history any time. Its live market commentary is written out in plain language so you can follow its reasoning in real time. Nothing is hidden behind a black box.

Why this is the moat

In a space crowded with screenshots of impossible returns and platforms that quietly vanish with people's deposits, the most valuable thing a trading product can offer isn't a flashier promise — it's the absence of one. NoVo's pitch is almost boring by comparison: we don't touch your money, and we don't hide what we're doing.

That honesty cuts both ways, and it should. Because your money never leaves your control, the outcomes — good and bad — are fully yours too. NoVo executes the trades; you still own the result, and you should understand what it's doing and accept that it has losing days like any real system. That's not a disclaimer we bury. It's the relationship: a powerful tool you control, not a black box you hope in.
