SPY options scalping, made easier: everything on short-dated SPY options, the brutal reality of the fill, and the dealer positioning under the levels - and why the fill, not the signal, decides the trade.

Short-dated SPY options - the 0-5 DTE window, and 0DTE (same-day) most of all - are the most leveraged, most time-sensitive, most execution-dependent corner of retail trading.
